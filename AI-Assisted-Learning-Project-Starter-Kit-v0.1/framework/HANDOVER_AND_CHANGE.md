## Handover

A substantial implementation handover should state:
- work or lab;
- status;
- outcome;
- files changed;
- files intentionally untouched;
- validation completed;
- validation deferred;
- evidence;
- open questions;
- next action;
- next owner.

## Change

Curriculum changes affect learning outcomes, architecture, intended scope, or material sequence and require Curriculum Architect involvement and learner approval.

Implementation changes remain within the approved specification.

Codex may report and propose corrections to defective or underspecified specifications but may not modify the learning contract.
