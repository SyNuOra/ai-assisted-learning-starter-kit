## Evidence types

### Implementation evidence
Use when claiming that something was implemented or validated.

Examples:
- working code;
- tests;
- runtime checks;
- configuration;
- generated artifacts;
- validation results.

### Learning evidence
Use when claiming that the learner understands or can apply a capability.

Examples:
- explanation;
- prediction;
- modification of a working example;
- independent variation;
- practical exercise;
- transfer to a new scenario;
- delayed retention check.

## Mini-evaluations

Use small targeted checks rather than formal exams.

Assess:
- what the learner can do independently;
- what is fragile;
- what is forgotten;
- what can be accelerated;
- what should be reinforced.

## Decision rule

Evidence → Decision → Next Action.

If evidence is insufficient, do not claim the outcome is achieved.
