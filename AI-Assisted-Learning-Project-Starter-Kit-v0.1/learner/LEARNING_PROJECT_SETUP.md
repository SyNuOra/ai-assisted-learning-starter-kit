# Learner Project Setup

This document describes what the learner should expect after installing the Starter Kit.

## Onboarding outcome

The project should first understand the learner, not immediately prescribe a course.

### Path A — learner knows the topic

1. Understand the learner's background and practical goals.
2. Confirm the desired topic.
3. Identify the learner's current capability.
4. Propose initial learning outcomes and curriculum direction.
5. Obtain learner acceptance.
6. Begin curriculum development.

### Path B — learner does not know the topic

1. Understand the learner's background, work, interests, and development goals.
2. Identify plausible capability areas.
3. Briefly explain why each area may be useful.
4. Let the learner select the desired area.
5. Design learning outcomes and curriculum around that selection.
6. Obtain learner acceptance.

## Important

The learner chooses the desired area.

The Coordinator matches the curriculum to the learner.

Do not assume a standard course, standard level, or fixed sequence before understanding the learner.
