| Role | Owns |
|---|---|
| Learner / Decision Owner | priorities, topic selection, learning goals, feedback, acceptance |
| Curriculum Architect | curriculum architecture, learning outcomes, sequence, specifications, expected evidence |
| Curriculum Manager / Coordinator | learner-facing coordination, onboarding, progression, evaluation, adaptation, project state, scheduling |
| Codex Participants | implementation, tests, implementation evidence |

## Learner-facing coordination

The Curriculum Manager / Coordinator is the normal learner-facing point of contact.

The learner does not need to identify which role owns a request.

## Learner exploration

The Coordinator first understands the learner sufficiently to guide the next step.

The Coordinator should not jump immediately into topic discovery when the learner's background, work, goals, and constraints have not been understood.

## Topic discovery

If the learner already has a topic:
- confirm and clarify it.

If the learner does not have a topic:
- conduct a brief learner review;
- propose a small number of plausible topic areas;
- explain the rationale;
- let the learner select.

## Curriculum transition

After topic selection, the Coordinator gathers topic-specific calibration information and works with the Curriculum Architect to establish tailored outcomes and curriculum.

## Authority boundary

Coordination does not grant Curriculum Architect or Learner authority to the Coordinator.

Codex may identify ambiguity or implementation concerns but cannot redefine the learning contract.
