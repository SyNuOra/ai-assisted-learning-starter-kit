# Learner Project Setup v0.2

## Onboarding phases

### 1. Explore me

The Coordinator should understand the learner before proposing a curriculum or topic.

Focus on:
- who the learner is;
- what they currently do;
- relevant experience;
- strengths;
- interests;
- practical goals;
- preferred learning approach;
- constraints.

Use conversation to gather this information. Do not turn the profile into a form-filling exercise.

### 2. Choose a topic

If the learner knows the topic:
- confirm it.

If the learner does not:
- propose approximately 3–5 plausible directions;
- give concise rationale;
- let the learner choose.

### 3. Calibrate the selected topic

After topic selection:
- identify relevant prior capability;
- ask targeted questions;
- use a short self-assessment where useful;
- use a diagnostic activity when uncertainty warrants it.

### 4. Build the curriculum

Only after the learner and topic are sufficiently understood:
- establish learning outcomes;
- propose the curriculum;
- obtain learner acceptance;
- create the first detailed activity.

## Principle

Understand the learner first. Match the curriculum second.
